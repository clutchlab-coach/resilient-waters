Serving Others with Purpose

Resilient Faith · Day 23 of 30 · By Isaac Bright Addo

“By love serve one another.”

— Galatians 5:13

Greatness in God's kingdom is measured differently than greatness in the world.

Service remains one of faith's highest expressions.

Today’s application

  • Do one act of service today that nobody will see.

Prayer

Lord, show me one person to serve today, and give me a willing heart.
