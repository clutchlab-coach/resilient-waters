The Importance of Community

Resilient Faith · Day 24 of 30 · By Isaac Bright Addo

“And let us consider one another to provoke unto love and to good works: not forsaking the assembling of ourselves together.”

— Hebrews 10:24–25

Faith was never intended to be lived in isolation.

Encouragement, accountability, and shared growth strengthen resilience.

Today’s application

  • Reach out to one believer today — encourage them, and let them encourage you.

Prayer

Lord, bind my life to a community that strengthens faith.
