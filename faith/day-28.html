Being a Light in the World

Resilient Faith · Day 28 of 30 · By Isaac Bright Addo

“Ye are the light of the world. Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father which is in heaven.”

— Matthew 5:14–16

A faithful life influences others through actions, kindness, and example.

Small acts of faithfulness often have lasting impact.

Today’s application

  • Do one visible act of kindness today, and let it point beyond you.

Prayer

Lord, let my life quietly point others to You.
