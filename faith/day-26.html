Stewardship of Creation

Resilient Faith · Day 26 of 30 · By Isaac Bright Addo

“And the LORD God took the man, and put him into the garden of Eden to dress it and to keep it.”

— Genesis 2:15

The natural world reflects God's creativity and generosity.

Caring for creation becomes an act of gratitude and stewardship.

This is where Resilient Faith meets Resilient Waters: the first job God gave humanity was to tend and keep a garden.

Today’s application

  • Care for one piece of creation today — plant, clean, repair, or simply waste less water.

Prayer

Lord, make me a grateful and careful steward of Your creation.
