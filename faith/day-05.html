The Difference Between Knowledge and Wisdom

Resilient Faith · Day 5 of 30 · By Isaac Bright Addo

“The fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom: and the knowledge of the holy is understanding.”

— Proverbs 9:10

Knowledge answers questions. Wisdom teaches how to live.

A person can possess degrees, expertise, and information while still lacking wisdom.

Biblical wisdom begins with humility and recognition that there is always more to learn.

Today’s application

  • Seek wisdom rather than merely information today.

Prayer

Lord, grant me wisdom for today's decisions.
