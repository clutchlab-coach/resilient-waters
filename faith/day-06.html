When the Future Feels Unknown

Resilient Faith · Day 6 of 30 · By Isaac Bright Addo

“For I know the plans I have for you, declares the LORD, plans to prosper you and not to harm you, plans to give you hope and a future.”

— Jeremiah 29:11

Few things create anxiety faster than uncertainty.

Yet God often reveals only enough light for the next step rather than the entire journey.

Faith is learning to trust the Guide when the map is incomplete.

Today’s application

  • Focus on the next faithful step rather than the entire future.
  • Write down one uncertainty and intentionally place it before God in prayer.

Prayer

Lord, when the path ahead feels unclear, help me trust Your wisdom, timing, and guidance.
