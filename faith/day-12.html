Faith During Waiting Seasons

Resilient Faith · Day 12 of 30 · By Isaac Bright Addo

“But they that wait upon the LORD shall renew their strength; they shall mount up with wings as eagles; they shall run, and not be weary; and they shall walk, and not faint.”

— Isaiah 40:31

Waiting remains one of life's most challenging experiences.

Yet Scripture repeatedly shows God working through seasons of waiting.

Growth often occurs quietly before results become visible.

Today’s application

  • Name one thing you are waiting for, and thank God for one thing He is growing in you while you wait.

Prayer

Lord, help me trust Your timing.
