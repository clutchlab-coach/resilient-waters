Technology, Distraction, and Spiritual Focus

Resilient Faith · Day 19 of 30 · By Isaac Bright Addo

“Martha, Martha, thou art careful and troubled about many things: but one thing is needful.”

— Luke 10:41–42

Many people have more digital connections than ever yet feel increasingly disconnected.

Spiritual focus requires intentional attention.

Attention is one of our most valuable resources — where it goes, the heart follows.

Today’s application

  • Put the phone in another room for one meal today.

Prayer

Lord, help me focus on what matters most.
