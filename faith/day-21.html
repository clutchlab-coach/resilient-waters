Hope for a Troubled World

Resilient Faith · Day 21 of 30 · By Isaac Bright Addo

“In the world ye shall have tribulation: but be of good cheer; I have overcome the world.”

— John 16:33

Christian hope is not optimism.

Optimism depends on circumstances. Hope depends on God's faithfulness.

Today’s application

  • Send one message of encouragement to someone facing a hard season.

Prayer

Lord, anchor my hope in Your faithfulness, not in circumstances.
