The Eternal Hope of Faith

Resilient Faith · Day 30 of 30 · By Isaac Bright Addo

“And God shall wipe away all tears from their eyes; and there shall be no more death, neither sorrow, nor crying, neither shall there be any more pain: for the former things are passed away.”

— Revelation 21:1–5

The Bible concludes with a vision of restoration.

Not merely individuals restored. Not merely communities restored. All creation renewed.

For believers, faith ultimately points beyond present struggles toward God's promise of redemption, renewal, and eternal life.

Everything under the sun has its season. Human achievements may fade, technologies may change, and earthly treasures may pass away. Yet faith invites us to invest in what endures: truth, hope, love, character, and a relationship with God.

Today’s application

  • Spend time reflecting on the difference between temporary concerns and eternal hope.

Prayer

Lord, help me live with an eternal perspective, trusting Your promises even when circumstances are difficult to understand.
