The Power of Gratitude

Resilient Faith · Day 22 of 30 · By Isaac Bright Addo

“In every thing give thanks: for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus concerning you.”

— 1 Thessalonians 5:18

Gratitude transforms how life is experienced.

What is appreciated is often enjoyed more fully.

Today’s application

  • Write down five gifts from this week, large or small, and thank God for each one.

Prayer

Lord, teach my heart to notice Your gifts and to say thank You.
