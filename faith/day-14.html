From Fear to Faith

Resilient Faith · Day 14 of 30 · By Isaac Bright Addo

“Fear thou not; for I am with thee: be not dismayed; for I am thy God: I will strengthen thee; yea, I will help thee; yea, I will uphold thee with the right hand of my righteousness.”

— Isaiah 41:10

Fear focuses on what might go wrong.

Faith focuses on who remains present regardless of what happens.

Today’s application

  • Write down one fear, then write Isaiah 41:10 beside it.

Prayer

Lord, replace my fear with confidence in Your presence.
