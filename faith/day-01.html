What Remains When Everything Else Changes?

Resilient Faith · Day 1 of 30 · By Isaac Bright Addo

“Jesus Christ is the same yesterday and today and forever.”

— Hebrews 13:8

Everything in life changes.

Technology that seems revolutionary today may become outdated tomorrow. Cities grow and decline. Careers shift unexpectedly. Health, finances, and circumstances can change in a moment.

Human beings naturally seek stability in unstable places. We often look for security in achievement, possessions, relationships, or status. Yet these foundations can be shaken.

Christian faith begins with the belief that God remains constant when everything else is changing.

Today’s application

  • Identify one area of life that feels uncertain.
  • Spend a few moments reflecting on God's unchanging character rather than the changing circumstances.

Prayer

Lord, when life feels uncertain, help me place my trust in what is eternal rather than what is temporary.
