The Gift of God's Peace

Resilient Faith · Day 7 of 30 · By Isaac Bright Addo

“And the peace of God, which passeth all understanding, shall keep your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus.”

— Philippians 4:6–7

Peace is not the absence of problems.

Biblical peace is the presence of God's assurance amid problems.

This peace does not depend on circumstances being favorable.

Today’s application

  • Replace worry with prayer whenever anxious thoughts arise today.

Prayer

Lord, guard my heart and mind with Your peace.
