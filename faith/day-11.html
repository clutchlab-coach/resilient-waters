The Courage to Keep Going

Resilient Faith · Day 11 of 30 · By Isaac Bright Addo

“Be strong and courageous. Do not be afraid; do not be discouraged, for the Lord your God will be with you wherever you go.”

— Joshua 1:9

There are moments when life asks us to move forward even when we feel uncertain.

Joshua understood this well. Standing on the edge of a new chapter, he faced responsibility, uncertainty, and challenges larger than himself.

Notice what God did not promise. He did not promise an easy journey. He did not promise an obstacle-free future. He did not promise instant success. Instead, God promised His presence.

Often the greatest act of faith is taking the next step without having every answer.

Today’s application

  • What step of faith are you postponing because of fear?
  • Take one small action today toward something God has placed on your heart.

Prayer

Lord, help me move forward with courage, trusting that Your presence is greater than my fears.
