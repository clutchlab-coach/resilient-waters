Faith That Endures

Resilient Faith · Day 29 of 30 · By Isaac Bright Addo

“I have fought a good fight, I have finished my course, I have kept the faith.”

— 2 Timothy 4:7

The Christian journey is not a sprint but a lifelong pilgrimage.

Endurance grows through daily faithfulness.

Today’s application

  • Take one small faithful step today — the same kind you can still be taking in ten years.

Prayer

Lord, keep me faithful for the whole journey, not just the easy miles.
