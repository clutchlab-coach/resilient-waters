Faith in an Age of Information Overload

Resilient Faith · Day 17 of 30 · By Isaac Bright Addo

“And be not conformed to this world: but be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind.”

— Romans 12:2

Never before has humanity had access to so much information.

Yet information abundance does not automatically produce wisdom.

Information alone cannot transform character. Renewal begins in the mind, and it is daily work.

Today’s application

  • Choose one hour today with no feeds or news — and spend ten of those minutes in Scripture or prayer.

Prayer

Lord, renew my mind through truth.
