Finding Truth in a Noisy World

Resilient Faith · Day 18 of 30 · By Isaac Bright Addo

“I am the way, the truth, and the life: no man cometh unto the Father, but by me.”

— John 14:6

Competing voices surround modern life.

Faith calls believers to seek truth with discernment, humility, and wisdom.

Discernment requires slowing down and seeking God.

Today’s application

  • Before believing or sharing one claim today, slow down and check it carefully.

Prayer

Lord, lead me into truth.
