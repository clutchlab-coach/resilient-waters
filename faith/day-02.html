Building Life on a Solid Foundation

Resilient Faith · Day 2 of 30 · By Isaac Bright Addo

“Therefore everyone who hears these words of mine and puts them into practice is like a wise man who built his house on the rock.”

— Matthew 7:24–27

Jesus compared two builders. Both built houses. Both experienced storms. Only one house stood.

The difference was not the severity of the storm but the strength of the foundation.

The same principle applies today. Difficulties are not evidence of failure. Storms are part of every life. The question is whether the foundation can endure when challenges arrive.

Today’s application

  • Ask yourself: what am I building my identity upon?
  • What remains if success, recognition, or comfort disappears?

Prayer

Lord, help me build my life on truth that can withstand life's storms.
