Resilience Through Prayer

Resilient Faith · Day 10 of 30 · By Isaac Bright Addo

“Men ought always to pray, and not to faint.”

— Luke 18:1

Prayer changes more than circumstances. Prayer changes perspective.

Through regular conversation with God, faith becomes stronger and fear becomes smaller.

Prayer develops resilience because it reminds us that we are not navigating life alone.

Today’s application

  • Set aside five uninterrupted minutes today for prayer.

Prayer

Lord, help prayer become a natural and constant part of my life.
