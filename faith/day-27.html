Living with Integrity

Resilient Faith · Day 27 of 30 · By Isaac Bright Addo

“What doth the LORD require of thee, but to do justly, and to love mercy, and to walk humbly with thy God?”

— Micah 6:8

Character is revealed not by public success but by private choices.

Integrity builds a life that remains strong over time.

Today’s application

  • Choose the honest option in one small decision today, even if no one would know.

Prayer

Lord, shape my private choices until they match my public words.
