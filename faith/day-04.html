Trusting God in Uncertain Times

Resilient Faith · Day 4 of 30 · By Isaac Bright Addo

“Trust in the LORD with all thine heart; and lean not unto thine own understanding. In all thy ways acknowledge him, and he shall direct thy paths.”

— Proverbs 3:5–6

Modern culture encourages complete self-reliance. Scripture teaches something different.

Trusting God does not mean abandoning responsibility or wisdom. It means acknowledging that human understanding has limits.

Many of life's most important questions cannot be solved through intellect alone.

Today’s application

  • Write down one concern and intentionally surrender it to God in prayer.

Prayer

Lord, teach me to trust You even when I cannot see what lies ahead.
