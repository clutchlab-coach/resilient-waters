Success, Ambition, and Eternal Perspective

Resilient Faith · Day 20 of 30 · By Isaac Bright Addo

“For what shall it profit a man, if he shall gain the whole world, and lose his own soul?”

— Mark 8:36

Success is not wrong.

The danger comes when temporary achievements replace eternal priorities.

Achievement has value, but it cannot replace purpose.

Today’s application

  • List three successes you are grateful for — and one lasting thing each one cannot give you.

Prayer

Lord, help me pursue what lasts.
