Why Does God Allow Difficulties?

Resilient Faith · Day 8 of 30 · By Isaac Bright Addo

“Count it all joy when ye fall into divers temptations; knowing this, that the trying of your faith worketh patience.”

— James 1:2–4

Difficult seasons often become growth seasons.

Scripture teaches that perseverance, character, and maturity frequently emerge through challenges rather than comfort.

Many qualities we admire most — courage, patience, compassion, wisdom — often emerge through difficulty.

Today’s application

  • Identify one lesson that recent difficulties may be teaching you.

Prayer

Lord, help me trust You even when I do not fully understand my circumstances.
