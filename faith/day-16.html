Artificial Intelligence and Human Purpose

Resilient Faith · Day 16 of 30 · By Isaac Bright Addo

“So God created man in his own image, in the image of God created he him; male and female created he them.”

— Genesis 1:27

Technology can process information.

Humans uniquely seek meaning, compassion, morality, worship, creativity, and relationship with God.

Machines may grow more capable, but human worth was never based on capability. It is based on being created in the image of God.

Today’s application

  • Do one thing today that no machine can do for you — encourage a person, in person or by hand.

Prayer

Lord, help me remember my true identity.
