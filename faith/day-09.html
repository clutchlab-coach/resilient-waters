Finding Strength in Weakness

Resilient Faith · Day 9 of 30 · By Isaac Bright Addo

“My grace is sufficient for thee: for my strength is made perfect in weakness.”

— 2 Corinthians 12:9

Human weakness often creates space for divine strength.

The areas we most want to hide may become places where God's grace is most visible.

Your limitations do not disqualify you. Sometimes they become the very places where grace shines brightest.

Today’s application

  • Invite God into one area where you need help today.

Prayer

Lord, remind me that Your strength is sufficient for every challenge.
