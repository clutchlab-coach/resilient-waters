Faith Beyond Feelings

Resilient Faith · Day 3 of 30 · By Isaac Bright Addo

“For we walk by faith, not by sight.”

— 2 Corinthians 5:7

Faith is not merely a feeling.

Feelings can fluctuate based on circumstances, health, stress, or disappointment. Faith involves choosing trust even when certainty is unavailable.

Many biblical heroes experienced seasons of doubt, fear, and confusion. Yet they continued moving forward.

Faith often grows strongest in seasons when feelings are weakest.

Today’s application

  • Consider an area where feelings are dominating your perspective. What would trusting God look like today?

Prayer

Lord, strengthen my faith beyond temporary emotions.
