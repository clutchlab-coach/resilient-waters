When God Feels Silent

Resilient Faith · Day 13 of 30 · By Isaac Bright Addo

“But I have trusted in thy mercy; my heart shall rejoice in thy salvation. I will sing unto the LORD, because he hath dealt bountifully with me.”

— Psalm 13

Many faithful people have experienced seasons when God seemed distant.

The Psalms remind readers that honest questions are not enemies of faith. They can become pathways to deeper trust.

Silence does not mean absence.

Today’s application

  • Read Psalm 13 aloud, and pray its closing verses in your own words.

Prayer

Lord, strengthen my faith during quiet seasons.
