Forgiveness and Freedom

Resilient Faith · Day 25 of 30 · By Isaac Bright Addo

“And be ye kind one to another, tenderhearted, forgiving one another, even as God for Christ's sake hath forgiven you.”

— Ephesians 4:32

Forgiveness does not excuse wrongdoing.

It releases the burden of carrying resentment.

Today’s application

  • Name one resentment you are carrying, and release it to God in prayer today.

Prayer

Lord, free my heart from resentment and teach me Your grace.
