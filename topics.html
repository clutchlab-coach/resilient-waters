The five series

  • Sustainability Explained — big ideas in plain words: the circular economy, carbon neutrality, ecosystem services, climate adaptation.
  • Future of Water — the future of drinking water, groundwater, wetlands and urban water systems.
  • Resilient Communities — case studies of cities and communities adapting well, including Indigenous practices and local innovation.
  • Nature at Work — how forests regulate water, how wetlands prevent flooding, how biodiversity supports farming and economies.
  • Research to Reality — recent scientific findings, including my own published research, translated into language anyone can use.

Reading order for new visitors: start with What Is Climate Adaptation and Why Does It Matter?, then follow whichever pillar touches your own life — your water, your town, your food, your coast.
